## 🌍 The Challenge of Distance & Timezones

Being in a long-distance relationship means navigating different schedules, continents, and timezones. Celebrating anniversaries or birthdays often feels incomplete when restricted to a video call or a standard text.

**[Heartreveals](https://heartreveals.com)** solves this with timezone-synchronized, interactive digital reveals:

1. **Synchronized Midnight Countdown**: Lock the surprise until midnight strikes in your partner's specific city (Paris, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, London, etc.).
2. **Interactive Memory Wall**: An evolving album of shared flights, visits, tickets, and photos.
3. **Secret Scratch Cards**: Virtual scratch-off cards revealing future flight tickets, reunion dates, or romantic promises.
4. **Romantic Couple Trivia**: Playful quizzes about memories only the two of you share.
5. **Atmospheric Soundtrack**: Soft background music accompanying your handwritten letter.
